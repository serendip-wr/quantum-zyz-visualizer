    MATHEMATICAL NOTES

    Why the decomposition works

    01

    Unitarity

    Quantum evolution preserves inner products and total probability, so a single-qubit gate obeys U†U = I. These 2×2 complex matrices form U(2).

    02

    U(2) → SU(2)

    Because det(U)=ei2δ, removing eiδ gives V=e−iδU with determinant 1. The phase has multiple valid branches, checked by reconstruction.

    03

    ZYZ Euler angles

    Every SU(2) matrix can be written as Rz(α)Ry(β)Rz(γ). At β=0 or π the angles are not unique; the tool fixes one redundant angle.

    04

    Right to left

    The rightmost matrix acts first, so the circuit runs Rz(γ) → Ry(β) → Rz(α). Global phase changes neither probabilities nor Bloch vectors.
